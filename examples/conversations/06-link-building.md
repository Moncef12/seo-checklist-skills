# Backlinks without getting penalized

**Skill:** `seo-links`. Claude talks the user out of bought links and sockpuppet accounts without preaching, then gives a concrete link plan, fastest wins first.

## You

> I need backlinks fast for my new project management SaaS. Thinking of buying a 500-backlink package on Fiverr and making a few Reddit accounts to recommend our tool in threads. Good plan? If not what should I do instead?

## Claude

No, I wouldn't do either one. Both are likely to cost you more than they get you.

### Why this plan will backfire

**The 500-backlink Fiverr package**
- These packages are nearly always links from spam blogs, private blog networks (PBNs, sites that exist only to sell links), comment spam and auto-generated profile pages. Google's spam policies treat bought links as "link schemes," and its SpamBrain system is built to spot them.
- The good outcome is that Google ignores them and you've wasted the money. The bad outcome is a manual action or an algorithmic hit on a new domain that has no trust to fall back on. Cleaning up afterwards, with disavow files and reconsideration requests, takes months.
- 500 links appearing on a brand-new site at once is itself a red flag.

**Fake Reddit accounts**
- New accounts that recommend a product get caught fast, both by Reddit's spam filters and by moderators. Project management subreddits see a lot of this and are quick to spot it.
- If someone connects the accounts to you, a thread like "[YourTool] is astroturfing Reddit" can end up ranking for your brand name. That's hard to undo.
- It's also against Reddit's rules and can get your domain banned site-wide.

### What to do instead (roughly fastest first)

**1. Directories and review sites (days).** Get listed on G2, Capterra, GetApp, Software Advice, SaaSHub, AlternativeTo, Product Hunt and BetaList, plus any directories for the tool categories you integrate with. Ask your first users for reviews. These links are relevant, fast and legitimate.

**2. Integration and partner pages.** If you integrate with Slack, Google Workspace, Zapier, GitHub and the like, apply for their app directories and marketplaces. These are high-authority links that are genuinely on-topic.

**3. "Best X" lists (the best return for a PM tool).** Search "best project management software," "[Asana/Trello/Monday] alternatives," "project management tools for [agencies/startups/remote teams]" and similar. Every page in the top 20 that doesn't list you is a target. Pitch the author with:
- a short description written in their list's format
- a screenshot
- a free account
- one clear angle, e.g. "the only PM tool that does X"

If a site wants payment, the link should be marked `rel="sponsored"`. That still brings referral traffic but doesn't help SEO.

**4. Backlink gap.** Run 3–5 competitors through Ahrefs Link Intersect or Semrush Backlink Gap. Look for sites that link to two or more of them but not to you. Those sites link to the category rather than to one company, so they're your warmest prospects.

**5. Reddit, done openly.** Search `site:reddit.com project management tool [your niche]` and look for threads that show up on Google's first page for your keywords. Answer the question properly from your real account, say "I work on X," and mention your tool only when it actually fits. Build a posting history on that account first. It's slower, but it lasts, and those threads increasingly get cited in AI answers too.

**6. Unlinked mentions (once you have any).** Set up a Google Alert for your brand name. When someone writes about you without linking, send them a short, friendly email asking for the link. Their acceptance rate is higher than for any other kind of outreach.

**7. Linkable assets (medium term).** A few things get linked to over and over in this space:
- free templates (sprint planning, RACI, project timeline)
- a small free tool, like a Gantt chart generator
- original data, e.g. "we analyzed 10k projects: here's how long tasks actually take"

A realistic goal for the first 60–90 days is 30–60 relevant links from sources like these. That will do far more for you than 500 spam links, and none of it can get you penalized.
