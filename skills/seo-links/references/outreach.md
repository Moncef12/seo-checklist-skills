# Outreach templates

Keep emails under 120 words, open with one specific detail from the target page, make one clear ask, and follow up once after 5-7 days. The [brackets] must be filled with real details. Never send a template with generic placeholders.

## Unlinked mention (L1)

**Subject:** Thanks for mentioning [Brand] in "[Article title]"

> Hi [Name],
>
> Just came across your piece on [topic]. [One specific, genuine reaction.] Thanks for including [Brand]!
>
> Would you be open to linking the mention to [URL]? It'd help readers who want to [benefit] find us directly.
>
> Either way, appreciate the shout-out.
>
> [Your name], [role] at [Brand]

## "Best X" list inclusion (L3)

**Subject:** A candidate for "[List title]"

> Hi [Name],
>
> Your list of [category] tools is one of the few that [specific compliment: actually tested them, has pricing tables...].
>
> I'd love for you to consider [Brand] for the next update. It's the [differentiator, e.g. "only one with native X"], used by [proof].
>
> To make it easy, here's a ready-made blurb in your format: [2-3 sentences]. Screenshot: [link]. Happy to set up a free account for testing.
>
> [Your name]

## Backlink gap: resource page (L2)

**Subject:** Resource suggestion for your [topic] page

> Hi [Name],
>
> I was using your [page title] page and noticed you link to [Competitor A] and [Competitor B] for [topic].
>
> We recently published [specific resource: original data, free tool, template] that covers [gap it fills]. It might be a useful addition: [URL].
>
> Thanks for maintaining the list,
> [Your name]

## Broken link (L1 reclaim / L2)

**Subject:** Broken link on [page title]

> Hi [Name],
>
> Quick heads-up: the link to [dead resource] in your [section] section returns a 404.
>
> If you're looking for a replacement, our [resource] covers the same thing: [URL]. Otherwise [alternative source] works too.
>
> [Your name]

## Follow-up (once)

> Hi [Name], just bumping this in case it got buried. No worries if it's not a fit. [Your name]
