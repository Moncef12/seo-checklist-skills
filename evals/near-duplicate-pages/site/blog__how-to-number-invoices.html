How to number your invoices

Each invoice you send must have its own number. The number lets you and your client refer to the same document, keeps your records in order, and is required by tax authorities in most countries. A good numbering system is simple, sequential and never reuses a number.

The most common format is a plain sequence such as 001, 002 and 003. Lots of freelancers put a prefix for the year, like 2026-001, so the sequence restarts each January and old invoices are easy to find. Agencies with several clients often add a client code, such as ACME-2026-004.

Pick one format and keep using it. Don't skip numbers, don't reuse a cancelled number, and keep the sequence going even when you change templates or software. If you cancel an invoice, issue a credit note that references the original number instead of deleting it.
