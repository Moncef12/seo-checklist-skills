Recurring invoices for retainers

Recurring invoices send themselves on a schedule, so retainer clients are billed on time every month without you lifting a finger. Set the amount, the start date and how often to bill, weekly, monthly or yearly, and Payly creates and sends each invoice automatically.

Clients can save a card or set up a bank debit so payments are collected on the due date. If a payment fails, Payly retries it and emails the client a secure link to update their details. You can pause, change or end a recurring invoice at any time, and every change is logged.

Recurring invoices work with every plan, support taxes and discounts, and show up in your cash flow forecast so you know exactly how much retainer income is coming in next month and next quarter. Combine them with automatic reminders for the clients who still pay by transfer.

Why freelancers switch to Payly: invoices go out in under a minute, clients pay by card or bank transfer from the invoice itself, reminders go out on their own when a payment is late, and you can see at a glance who owes you money and how much is coming in this month.

Payly is invoicing software for freelancers and small studios. Send invoices, take card payments, chase late clients automatically and see who owes you what, all in one place. Made in Austin, Texas. Terms, privacy and security.
