A successful response:
- Proposes at least one rewritten title that contains the keyword (or a close variant) and states its character count, with the title between 50 and 60 characters.
- Proposes a rewritten meta description of roughly 120-160 characters that is specific (a benefit or proof), not generic.
- Flags that there is no H1 (the only heading is an H2) and proposes an H1.
- Flags that the opening sentence does not answer the query and rewrites it so the first line states what the product does for freelancers.
- Flags the image missing alt text (and ideally suggests WebP/AVIF and dimensions).
- Gives concrete paste-ready rewrites, not just generic advice.
